# 10. Limitations

Every limitation below is taken from the programme's own registrations and
deviation logs rather than assembled for this section, and each one names what
it does and does not put at risk.

1. **The placebo arm has not been run.** Experiment 4 cannot separate the
   content of the counter line from the presence of an extra sentence of
   feedback. The registration recorded this before the run and the secondary
   analysis written to bear on it does not settle it. It is the largest open
   question in the paper, and the mechanism proposed in Section 9 depends on it.

2. **One model carries three of the four experiments.** Experiments 2, 3 and 4
   are all the same frontier model, so every elimination rests on one model. The
   capability result in Section 5 is three models, and it is the only claim here
   that is not single-model.

3. **One provider, and one snapshot of it.** Nothing here is replicated across
   providers, and a different frontier model could in principle behave
   differently at the decision point.

4. **The decisive experiment rests on 71 paired tasks.** Effects in this
   programme live in counts of single digits, which is why every test is exact
   and why no confidence interval appears anywhere. A thirteen-against-one split
   is a strong result at this size and would still be a small sample at any
   other.

5. **Two of the comparisons cross a day boundary.** Experiment 3's arm ran the
   day after its control. The churn floor calibrates this once, at 2 tasks of
   71, but it says what a third identical run would probably do rather than what
   a different day would do, and it was measured only in the same session.

6. **The corpus is generated, and its conventions come from a catalogue.**
   Whether the same failure appears on organic under-specification in real
   repositories is untested, and the guarantee that makes this instrument work
   is exactly the guarantee a real repository cannot give.

7. **Experiment 1's registration did not partition the outcome space.** One
   prediction is recorded as neither confirmed nor disconfirmed because its two
   clauses left a gap and the run landed in it. Later registrations fixed this
   by requiring three clauses that partition.

8. **Episodes that never came back are dropped rather than counted.** Four in
   Experiment 2 and one each elsewhere failed in transport, and each is excluded
   from the paired statistics rather than scored as a failure, because an absent
   episode is not an unsolved one.

9. **Operational scars are logged rather than smoothed.** One publish gate was
   waived and recorded verbatim as a deviation. And a hash taken over a
   directory listing rather than over a named file set meant that two pooled
   figures in Experiment 2 print differently once later result files exist. The
   published values are quoted here and the population they are taken over is
   named, and no verdict moves either way.
