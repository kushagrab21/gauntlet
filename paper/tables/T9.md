**Caption.** All five sets of rules restricted to the same 72 tasks, so the rows describe the same work. **These rows are a description and not a comparison.** Three of them were played on one day and two on another, and a difference between two rows from different days cannot be told apart from a difference between the two days. The comparisons this paper draws are the task-by-task ones in Tables 4, 5 and 6, each of which reads only tasks played under both sets of rules being compared. **The table is included so that the five sets of rules can be seen side by side, and no inference is drawn from any difference between its rows.**
