# T4 — Experiment 2: telling the model how many stages are hidden does not change what it does

| what the model was told it could do | tasks played | tasks where it said DONE at the decision point | of those, how many were wrong | share of tasks where it said DONE | tasks it claimed and the other did not |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| may say DONE (control) (CTRL) | 118 | 71 | 21 | 0.6017 | 6 |
| told how many stages are hidden (WARN) | 116 | 69 | 19 | 0.5948 | 6 |

**Caption.** Both sets of rules were played over the same tasks, so the comparison is task by task rather than total against total. **115 tasks were played under both.** On 63 the model said DONE at the decision point under both sets of rules and on 40 it said DONE under neither; those 103 tasks carry no information about a difference and the registered test ignores them. That leaves 12 tasks that changed answer, split 6 against 6. The **share of tasks where it said DONE** column is the count of tasks with at least one DONE at the decision point divided by the tasks played in the column to its left; it is a description, and the registered test reads only the tasks that changed answer. The one-sided exact McNemar p-value in the registered direction is **p = 0.6128**.  **Six tasks moved each way, so telling the model the exact number of hidden stages it had not seen did not reduce how often it claimed the task was finished.**
